### Features

- Adds optional `SidebarDefinition.scopeRoot?: "configured" | "editorial"` (default `"editorial"`, unchanged behavior). `"configured"` caps Broaden, branch focus, Restore, and route reconciliation at the authored forest: empty `items` stop at the category-filtered tree, one `autogenerated` directory stops at that directory, and an explicit list stops at that list. `initialPath` remains the starting view and the Restore baseline. At the cap the scope toolbar is omitted. An unsupported value fails the build. An omitted entry or an array stays editorial. (418b2f29c)

### Bug Fixes

- The desktop sidebar and the mobile drawer each keep their scroll offset across client navigation, a full document load, reload, and Back/Forward, including after Broaden. Restoration runs after the destination tree can hold the offset, so a replaced sidebar or a temporary empty tree does not snap back to the top. A saved zero is restored. A missing snapshot is not written as zero. A closed drawer keeps its saved offset in history until it opens again. Article scroll, focus, filtering, and disclosure are left unchanged. (418b2f29c)
