### Other Changes

- Requires zfb 4.3.3: the `@takazudo/zfb`, `zfb-runtime` and `zfb-md-wasm` peer floors move from `^4.3.2` to `^4.3.3`. zfb 4.3.3 moves its YAML frontmatter parser past [RUSTSEC-2026-0333](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0333); frontmatter accepted by 4.3.2 is still accepted. Upgrade the zfb family (and `zfb-adapter-cloudflare` when used) to 4.3.3 together with this release. (bbc70837e)
